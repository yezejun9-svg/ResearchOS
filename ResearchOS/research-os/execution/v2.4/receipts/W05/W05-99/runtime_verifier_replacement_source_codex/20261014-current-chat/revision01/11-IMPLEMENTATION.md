# F1/F2 revision: author evidence, independent acceptance pending

F1 uses a persisted plan-v2 `history_sequence` and predecessor operation ID, exact plan SHA256 and terminal event hash. Every immutable terminal, including ABORTED, forms the next head. Startup checks the complete contiguous causal chain and the current registration against its final head. Commit compares the planned predecessor with the freshly read head both before journal creation and immediately before publication. Recover validates that the target extends the other terminal history. Events retain their sequence/previous/hash checks; their RFC3339Nano audit timestamps are parsed but never used for order or authority.

This makes differing timestamp precision, equal timestamps and clock rollback safe without editing prior history. An A-to-B-to-A registration byte cycle does not revalidate a previously prepared plan: its predecessor binding remains stale. Forks, cycles, missing predecessors, sequence gaps and predecessor digest drift fail closed. Plans from the earlier draft v1 schema lack this binding and are rejected, with no migration or rewriting of historical events. No actual product history was read or changed.

F2 enumerates reserved `local-verifier-` principals independently of mutable root/parent/grants. It validates every root, child, derived child ID and grant, plus orphan reserved grants and lineage. Only structurally complete other service roots and their children are legitimate foreign identities. Their revoked state may be retained, but all reserved identities enter the snapshot digest; reparenting an existing current child to a legitimate foreign root still fails the derived-ID check. Current registration authority still requires the original active root, Owner, host and SID.

Regression evidence includes the original independent review's exact three probes via its unchanged overlay; three timestamp scenarios with forward/reverse; six causal-corruption cases; a byte-roundtrip stale-plan case; eight child-negative cases with two fixture children; and successful replacement with intact active/revoked foreign roots followed by reparent rejection. All eight native Windows kill boundaries also verify that the recovered terminal is bound by and permits a new operation. Existing CLI, CAS-race, ACL/reparse, readonly census/WAL and startup-before-store tests are retained.
